MovementSpeed = Movement Points
To move from 1 Hex to Another costs 1 Movement.
If that elevation is higher than your current it costs +1 Movement per elevation.
So if the hex is 1 elevation higher than your current it will cost 2 movement.
If moving from 1 hex to another involves crossing a river it costs +1 Movement.

Line of Sight - LOSLOS is how far your unit can see. Each planet is different. Some are dusty or have bad weather
and have very limited Max LOS. Some are clear so LOS is much higher.
Normal LOS is 3-4 Hex Range for a sandard unit.

Line of sight is literally a line from the center of your unit to the target. If nothing that blocks LOS
is in the way. Your unit can see said unit inside the Max LOS of said planet.

Some gear and equipment like optics can expand LOS in different ways.
Things thats block LOS

Terrain in the way. Buildings or other large structures including player made structures. 2 Hex's of Forest.

Hex PostionHex's in the meta campaign are devided into 3 sections.
Each Section can hold 1 unit. For a max of 3 units per hex.
Not all Hex's can hold a full 3 units. If a building or immpassable terrain for example is taking up
1/3 of a hex then that hex can only hold 2 units. If a building or immpassible terrain or water takes
up up to 2/3rds of a hex then it can only hold 1 unit. IF IN DOUBT. ASSUME IT CAN'T HOLD A UNIT

When moving into a hex, The first combat infantry unit to enter the hex will take up a forward postion in
the direction they designated. The next unit will take up the next spot and the next.
